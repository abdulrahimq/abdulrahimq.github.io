About Me

I started my PhD in Linguistics at Stony Brook in 2024
Advisor: Owen Rambow
Email: abdelrahim.qadoumi stonybrook edu
Office: Ward Melville Social and Behavioral Sciences Building (SBS), N222

I focus on computational linguistics. My research combines formal language theory, phonology, and machine learning to understand the capabilities and limitations of language models, especially transformers.

Previously, I've worked on projects related to Arabic dialect phonology, finite-state automata, and morphophonemics. I'm also interested in historical Arabic linguistics and the linguistic heritage of Arab diaspora communities in NYC.
